Role brief

Head of Partnership Development

The person who turns Marblism's partners into a community, our community into events, and our events into new members of the affiliate program.

Growth team Remote + in person First 90 days defined
The mission

Grow the number of partners selling Marblism, keep them motivated every week, and put Marblism in every room where business owners are listening.

What you'll own

Four pillars

Everything in this role ladders up to one outcome: more partners in the program, more active, earning more.

01

Community

  • Run and moderate our Facebook and LinkedIn community groups: posts, replies, welcome threads, keeping the conversation alive.
  • Manage the WhatsApp groups where partners get updates, wins and quick answers.
02

Events & webinars

  • Organize events with our top partners, online and in person.
  • Plan, promote and host company webinars end to end: topic, speakers, registration, run of show and follow-up.
03

Affiliate growth

  • Increase partners' affiliate commissions by coaching and motivating them on weekly calls.
  • Host the weekly partner update call: program changes, new offers, what's working.
  • Push and promote the Affiliate Collective program.
04

PR & representation

  • Drive digital PR campaigns and activations: podcast bookings, media outlet interviews.
  • Represent Marblism publicly with sharp copywriting and full professionalism.
The program you'll scale

Affiliate Collective

Marblism's multi-level affiliate model: partners earn on the customers they bring in, and also on the partners they recruit into the program.

Your job is to make it the obvious move for anyone in our community: explain it clearly, recruit into it, and help every level of the network sell more.

The bigger the collective, the bigger everyone's check.

Months 1 to 3

First 90 days

Fast adaptation is the expectation: learn the program in weeks, not months, and start moving numbers right away.

Month 1

Learn and take the mic

  • Take over the community groups and the WhatsApp channels.
  • Host the weekly partner update calls from week one.
  • Lock the date, theme and partners for webinar #1.
Month 2

First big webinar

  • Deliver webinar #1 with 500+ registrants, solo or co-hosted with community members.
  • Launch the Affiliate Collective push across every channel.
  • Book the first podcasts and media interviews.
Month 3

Repeat and scale

  • Webinars #2 and #3, each with 500+ registrants.
  • More people in the program, higher engagement in every group and call.
  • First in-person event with top partners on the calendar.
